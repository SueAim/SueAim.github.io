# Company and career-pathway research

Reviewed October 28, 2026. Designed first for **AIM New Jersey students without A&P**. This is a maintained research record, not an exhaustive list of every US airline or shop.

## How to read the results

- Scholarship dollars, tuition paid to schools, tools, wages and training are different benefits.
- Conditional employment depends on stated requirements. A guaranteed interview or recruiting relationship does not guarantee a job.
- A public program page or historical press release does not prove a current vacancy.
- NJ residence and continuing at AIM NJ are different questions. Other-school routes remain available in optional filters, hidden from the default student view.
- No one needs an existing A&P just to browse. Some programs require FAA certification before the eventual job starts.

## Most relevant career leads to check with AIM NJ

1. **Republic RJet Maintenance Cadet:** current provider listing describes 400 hours of A&P study, year-round interviews and conditional employment. It says a 2- or 4-year program; AIM confirms a partnership, but the 21-month NJ course needs explicit confirmation. No binding cadet commitment is described.
2. **British Airways US Engineering Cadet:** provider explicitly requires enrollment at a US AIM program. Current recruitment, sponsorship coverage, NJ participation and the 21-month course need confirmation. BA describes US stations and short London trips; AIM summarizes the London phase differently.
3. **United / AIM technician pathway:** mentorship and streamlined recruiting; no universal offer or transferable scholarship established.
4. **SkyWest pathway:** guaranteed interview subject to requirements and conditional exam reimbursement; not paid employment while studying. Verify current school eligibility.

## Remaining research

Continue checking regional airlines, local Part 145 shops, component/engine suppliers, professional associations and employer-sponsored awards administered through shared portals. Many small-company awards are administered by AEA, AWAM, WAI, AMC/PAMA, Choose Aerospace or a partner school rather than a company’s own application page. Avoid counting the same award twice.

AWAM’s current page says the 2026 season is closed and the 2027 window is TBA. Old sponsor lists are leads, not evidence that each award will repeat. ADMA currently names management/pilot degree criteria despite historical A&P recipients. NetJets’ old SkillPointe maintenance scholarship ended in 2023; its separate Columbus Foundation award needs certificate-school confirmation.

Daily automation checks the maintained provider pages for reachability and changes. It does not independently discover every new company program or re-verify each scholarship’s eligibility daily.
