# Ongoing scholarship research requirements

Owner's standing instruction, recorded October 28, 2026.

## Priority

Actively discover and add new applicable opportunities whenever they exist. Do not restrict research to the catalog's current source URLs or repeatedly check only major scholarship organizations. Keep expanding the search across smaller companies and previously unchecked provider sites.

The primary audience is students CURRENTLY attending AIM New Jersey's 21-month Part 147 program who have not yet earned A&P. Nationwide awards that accept NJ applicants qualify. A donor's address outside NJ does not by itself make an award ineligible.

## Search coverage

Include large and small/regional/private airlines, cargo operators, independent MROs and Part 145 repair stations, aircraft-parts suppliers/distributors, engine/component/avionics/electrical repair shops, tool companies, manufacturers, employer foundations, industry associations and school-administered company awards. Rotate through new companies, sectors, regions and search terms; follow useful leads outside the existing list.

Include cash scholarships, tuition support, reimbursement, testing fees, free tools/toolboxes, paid training, internships and apprenticeships. Give particular attention to scholarship-linked conditional job offers, guaranteed interviews, placement/referrals and employment-linked education. Verify the precise benefit and any conditions; a recruiting contact or interview is not guaranteed employment.

Use current primary provider/application evidence. Directories and public company social posts can help discover opportunities, but do not establish eligibility or an open application by themselves. Record newly checked companies, source URLs, dates and findings in research-coverage.md so the search universe expands over time.

## Publication and accuracy

Add verified applicable discoveries to the existing catalog and publish them. Deduplicate shared applications and repeated provider announcements. Label payment routes, demographic and prior-degree requirements, program/campus restrictions, relocation, work commitments and repayment terms accurately. Keep other-school and degree-only routes out of the default AIM NJ view.

Uncertain finds may appear only as clearly labeled unverified leads. Never invent a daily addition, call an old announcement newly launched, roll an expired deadline forward, or assume a named award repeats merely because the provider has an annual program.

Maintain automatic expiry cleanup, recurring-cycle watches, 404/410 hiding and recovery, transient-error distinctions, official source links and genuine check timestamps. Keep hidden URLs in the recovery set.

Keep the mobile interface compact and focused on open opportunities, prominent award amounts and clear benefit types. Preserve privacy/no-registration behavior. Do not introduce paid services or expose student data.
