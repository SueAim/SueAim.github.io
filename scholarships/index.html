Privacy should be easy to understand

This static preview has no forms, accounts, tracking scripts or AI connection. It does not collect application backgrounds or essays. The hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its policies.

Our design for the application service

Guest-first use, temporary processing of application details, and your approval before information is sent to a scholarship provider. AI and voice processing will be explained before you use them.

Optional accounts and email alerts will require limited information and deletion/unsubscribe controls. Deleting information here cannot delete copies already submitted to scholarship providers.

Replies will use your personal email where the provider supports email, or your account on the provider's portal.

Independent student project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Aviation Institute of Maintenance. Scholarship providers determine eligibility and awards.
