    YOU DON’T NEED TO BE TECH-SAVVY

    Three small steps forward.

    01

    Find your kind of support

    Choose your location, then cash, tuition, tools or training. Add eligibility filters only if they help.

    02

    Check the real requirements

    Read the details and official source. Confirm your campus, program, deadline and how the award is paid.

    03

    Apply on your terms

    Use your own browser and personal email. The provider may require an account; this directory doesn’t.

    Can I use ChatGPT Free for essays?

    Where a provider permits AI assistance, use your real experiences, goals and voice, then review the result. ChatGPT Free has usage limits. This directory does not generate essays or pay for API usage. Cloud-browser access depends on your plan.

    Open ChatGPT ↗ · Check browser availability ↗

    What if a provider prohibits AI writing?

    Write the application yourself and follow its rules. More personal input, a different browser or a PDF export does not make AI-written text acceptable. If the rule is unknown, confirm before using assistance.

    Are these all available scholarships?

    No directory can guarantee complete coverage. This researched catalog includes open awards, future cycles and labeled leads. We search nationwide because awards outside NJ may accept NJ students. Additional directories below can help you keep looking.

    Will it work on my phone or computer?

    The responsive page is designed for current Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows and Mac browsers. Full screen requires a tap and browser support. The page works normally without it. Use the Aa button for larger text.

    A SIMPLE PRIVACY PROMISE

    Your search.
    Your business.

    No account. No application uploads. No saved student profile. Filters and shortlist stay in page memory. No advertising trackers or analytics scripts are installed.

    GitHub Pages may process ordinary request information, such as your IP address. Opening a scholarship provider or ChatGPT is your choice; those services have their own privacy policies.

    This version has no accounts or email subscriptions to delete. resets filters and shortlist. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them. We cannot delete applications already sent to a provider.

    Optional sign-in, email alerts, voice and AI are not connected in this first version. Public scholarship updates work independently of those tools.
