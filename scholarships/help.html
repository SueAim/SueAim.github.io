Help & privacy

How to use this site

Search or choose a support type. Open Details to check eligibility and the official application. Save useful opportunities to your shortlist.

The Money awards filter includes student payments, tuition support, reimbursement and exam-fee grants. Free tools has its own filter. Payment routes and wages are labeled separately. A conditional job offer or interview has requirements; it is not an unconditional job guarantee.

This free directory does not currently generate essays, autofill forms or send email alerts.

Can I use ChatGPT Free for essays?

Where a provider permits AI assistance, use your real experiences, goals and voice, then review the result. ChatGPT Free has usage limits. This directory does not generate essays or pay for API usage. Cloud-browser access depends on your plan.

What if a provider prohibits AI writing?

Write the application yourself and follow its rules. More personal input, a different browser or a PDF export does not make AI-written text acceptable. If the rule is unknown, confirm before using assistance.

Are these all available scholarships?

No directory can guarantee complete coverage. This researched catalog includes open awards, future cycles and labeled leads. We search nationwide because awards outside NJ may accept NJ students. Use “More official scholarship sources” at the bottom of the scholarship page to keep looking.

Will it work on my phone or computer?

The responsive page is designed for current Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows and Mac browsers. Full screen requires a tap and browser support. The page works normally without it. Use the Aa button for larger text.

Privacy · No account or saved profile

No sign-up, personal uploads, advertising trackers or saved student profiles. Filters and shortlist stay in page memory. Reloading or closing the scholarship page clears its choices.

GitHub Pages and linked providers have their own privacy policies. Downloads remain on your device until you delete them. Applications sent directly to providers are under their control.

Expired one-time awards and confirmed missing links are hidden automatically. Recurring awards remain on a next-cycle watch. Hidden source links are rechecked for recovery.
