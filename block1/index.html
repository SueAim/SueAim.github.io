Four forces pull on the airplane — two tug-of-wars: LIFT vs WEIGHT (up vs down) and THRUST vs DRAG (forward vs back). All four are measured in pounds (lb). Press a button and watch which arrow wins.
4 Forces Lift · Weight · Thrust · Drag
Lift fights weight and thrust fights drag. When each pair is equal, the airplane holds steady (balanced forces). Tap any arrow — or the little green pair under the wing — to learn more.
NEWTON’S 3rd LAW — equal & opposite action and reaction (say it LOUD!). The same law works all three primary flight controls:
Every push gets an equal push back the other way. The wing pushes the air DOWN → the air pushes the wing UP — that reaction is LIFT. And each control: AILERON up → that wing DOWN; ELEVATOR up → tail DOWN → nose UP; RUDDER left → tail RIGHT → nose LEFT. Every one of these pairs is an action and its equal-and-opposite reaction.

The WING itself, sliced through = the AIRFOIL — NOT the aileron (the hinged flap on the trailing edge: aileron up kills lift → that wing drops; slider up here = MORE LIFT → this wing rises). CAMBER = the curved top (green dashes). ANGLE OF ATTACK = chord line vs RELATIVE WIND — raise it, lift grows; too steep → STALL, kills lift.
BERNOULLI: the PRESSURE DIFFERENCE creates lift. NEWTON’S 3rd LAW: the wing pushes air DOWN = ACTION; the air pushes the wing UP = REACTION — equal & opposite. Both describe the same LIFT.
Dihedral Why the wings angle UP — lateral stability
Why it works (press ▶): ① A gust BANKS the airplane. ② The tilted lift lets it SLIP sideways toward the low wing. ③ Because of the DIHEDRAL (upward wing angle), that side-air meets the LOW wing more from below → its ANGLE OF ATTACK rises (the high wing’s falls). ④ Bigger AoA = MORE LIFT on the low wing — the camber never changes; it is the angle that changes. ⑤ Unequal lift rolls it back LEVEL — LATERAL STABILITY, about the longitudinal axis.
AoA is measured against the AIR, not the ground. Gust → BANK → SLIP toward the low wing → side-air hits the LOW wing’s BOTTOM (tip tilts up = DIHEDRAL) → its ANGLE OF ATTACK rises → MORE LIFT → rolls back LEVEL — LATERAL STABILITY. High wing: air across the top → smaller AoA → less lift. Camber never changes. (Like wind under a tilted hand out a car window.)
